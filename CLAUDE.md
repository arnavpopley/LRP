# Instructions for Claude in this repo

This is Arnav's ME3 Literature Review Project (LRP 241, GWP of hydrogen). Deadline 12:00, 11 Dec 2026; report main body max 18 pages.

## Hard rules

- **Never write report text.** Nothing that could be pasted into `report/`. The module forbids copying AI-generated content into the report, and Arnav's own rule limits AI to screening, extraction, and challenging his analysis. Critique of his drafts is fine; rewritten paragraphs are not.
- **Every number needs a source and a page.** Take figures from the PDF in `papers/`, not from abstracts, press releases, or memory. If a value is only from an abstract or web read, say so.
- **Log AI use.** Any AI-assisted task gets a row in the `AI log` sheet of `tracker/research_tracker.xlsx`, including errors caught.
- **Don't add papers without asking.** The source list in `extraction/paper_list.txt` is frozen unless he agrees.

## Conventions

- PDFs: `papers/year_FirstAuthor_journal_topic.pdf`.
- New extractions go into `extraction/extraction.txt` under the right objective, with page numbers, and conflicts go in the list at the top.
- Keep `extraction/references.ris` in step with `paper_list.txt`.
- Maths in LaTeX; Oxford commas; blunt, short answers.
